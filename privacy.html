Privacy & data controls

Your health data
is personal.

This policy describes Verve, a personal-use iPhone health app, and its private Google Health integration. The Google Health connection is currently in development; authorizing it does not mean automatic in-app synchronization is already available.

Effective September 4, 2026.

Information Verve accesses

Verve stores information you choose to enter, including food logs, nutrition targets, workout plans, and fridge items. With permission, it reads Apple Health records such as sleep, workouts, steps, body measurements, heart rate, and related measurements. Photos, receipt images, and text are processed when you use the corresponding food or coaching features.

The optional Google Health connection requests only read access to sleep and health metrics and measurements. These permissions cover sleep sessions and stages, heart rate and resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, oxygen saturation, respiratory rate, temperature-related measurements, and body measurements where available. Verve uses relevant records to support personal sleep and health review. It does not request permission to write or delete Google Health records.

How information is used

Health information is used to display your records, summarize sleep and measurements, and support the health features you choose to use. It is not sold, used for advertising, used for credit or insurance decisions, or published on this website. Verve does not use Google Health information to train generalized AI models.

Verve's use of information received from Google Health API and Developer Tools adheres to the Google Health API Developer and User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, and the Google API Services User Data Policy.

Storage and service providers

App records are stored in your device's files and your own iCloud storage. Some Apple Health exports currently depend on iCloud Drive. These files can contain sensitive information and should not be shared publicly.

Google stores the upstream Fitbit/Google Health records under your Google account. The private integration retrieves authorized records over HTTPS. The planned Verve relay uses Cloudflare Workers to process requests transiently, with OAuth credentials held in Cloudflare's encrypted secrets storage rather than in public source code. It is not intended to maintain a server-side health database. During setup, developer-controlled local tools may temporarily handle authorization credentials and data for verification.

Separate food-analysis and coaching features use Cloudflare and configured AI providers, including Google, OpenAI, or DeepSeek. Selected request content may be transmitted to those providers to fulfill the feature. The Google Health connection itself does not send retrieved health records to an AI provider. Any future sharing of Google Health records for coaching must be clearly disclosed and authorized before it occurs.

Infrastructure providers may process operational metadata, such as IP addresses, request times, and security information under their own policies. Access to health data is limited to operating the features you authorize, security needs, or legal requirements.

Security and retention

Verve relies on device security, protected cloud storage, encrypted transport, and restricted credential access. Keep your device protected with a passcode and your Apple and Google accounts secured. No system can guarantee absolute security.

App files and iCloud copies remain until you delete them. Google retains its upstream records according to your Google account settings. Authorization credentials are retained while the private connection is enabled and must be removed when it is disconnected. Temporary setup credentials are removed after configuration. Operational metadata may be retained by infrastructure providers under their policies.

Disconnecting and deleting data

  • Revoke Verve's Google access in Google Account connections. This stops future authorized retrieval; it does not erase copies already imported.
  • Change Apple Health permissions in the Health app. Revoking access does not automatically remove existing exports.
  • Delete Verve's exported files from iCloud Drive and remove local app data when you want to erase retained copies. Removing the iPhone app alone may leave iCloud copies and backups.
  • For the private Google integration, remove its stored OAuth secrets and any retained local exports as part of disconnection. There is currently no public multi-user account or self-service server deletion portal.
  • Deleting data in Verve does not delete the original records in Google Health or Apple Health. Manage those records in their respective apps.

This website

This static site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no login form, health-data upload, advertising, analytics script, or application credentials. GitHub may collect technical information associated with serving the site; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact and changes

The Verve maintainer can be reached through the public site issue tracker for general privacy questions or to request a private contact method. Do not post health records, tokens, personal identifiers, or other sensitive information there. Material changes to data access or use will be reflected in this policy and, where required, presented for consent before taking effect.
