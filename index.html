Log what you eat, plan your training, and understand your rest. Verve brings your daily health information together without an advertising feed or a public health profile.

Eat

Keep food logs, track nutrition, and organize your fridge.

Train

Sleep

Review measured sleep duration, stages, and trends.

Your records, under your control.

Verve uses on-device files and your own iCloud storage. Apple Health access is permission-based. Network-backed food and coaching features process the information needed for those requests.

A private, read-only Google Health integration is in development for Fitbit sleep and health measurements. Google access is optional and requires your authorization. It does not change records in your Google account.

Verve is currently a personal-use app, not a publicly available service or medical device.
